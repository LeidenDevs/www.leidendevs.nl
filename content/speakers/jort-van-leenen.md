Jort van Leenen is CEO of LUdev, the student-run software organisation at Leiden University's computer science institute ([LIACS](https://liacs.leidenuniv.nl/)), where he leads the organisation and its collaborations with external clients. He previously served as CTO, overseeing technical infrastructure and supporting student teams through a full project cycle. He is currently an MSc student in Computer Science at Leiden University.
