An introduction to the [Tech Workers Coalition Netherlands](https://www.techwerkers.nl/). A community of tech workers doing work place organizing, aimed at taking back worker power in the Netherlands.

From engineers and delivery drivers to content moderators, customer service agents, warehouse pickers and video game artists; anyone in a tech adjacent role or workplace is welcome to join.
